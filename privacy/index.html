Privacy Policy

Grow Your Home — iOS edition

Developer: Uladzislau Andreyeu
Effective date: August 3, 2026
Contact: support@playgrowyourhome.com

Grow Your Home is a free offline-friendly iPhone game. The iOS edition does not require an account and does not include advertising, in-app purchases, analytics, or tracking.

Data we collect

The developer does not collect personal data or gameplay data from the iOS edition of Grow Your Home.

Data stored on your device

Garden progress, inventory, achievements, language, and sound settings are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. This information is used only to restore your game. It is not sent to the developer or third parties.

You can clear this information with the Reset Progress control in the game. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data from the device.

Device permissions and third parties

The iOS edition does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos. It does not include advertising, analytics, payment, attribution, or social-network SDKs.

Apple may process information when it distributes the app or provides App Store and TestFlight services. Apple's handling of that information is governed by Apple's own privacy policy and is not controlled by Grow Your Home.

Children

Grow Your Home is not submitted as a Made for Kids app. The iOS edition does not knowingly collect personal data from children or adults.

Changes and contact

If this policy or the app's data practices change, the published policy and App Store privacy answers will be updated before the changed version is released. Privacy questions can be sent to support@playgrowyourhome.com.

Какие данные мы собираем

Разработчик не собирает персональные данные или данные игрового процесса из iOS-версии Grow Your Home.

Данные на устройстве

Прогресс сада, инвентарь, достижения, язык и настройки звука хранятся локально в закрытом хранилище приложения на устройстве. Они используются только для восстановления игры и не передаются разработчику или третьим лицам.

Эти данные можно очистить командой сброса прогресса в игре. При удалении приложения его локальные данные удаляются с устройства.

Grow Your Home не подаётся в категорию Made for Kids. iOS-версия не собирает персональные данные ни детей, ни взрослых.
